The ritual

From meadow to mantel, slowly.

Gathered at dawn

Flowers are cut in the early morning, when the petals hold the most moisture and colour.

Pressed for three weeks

Layered between cotton sheets in wooden presses, changed weekly to hold their hue.

Set by hand

Each piece is arranged and sealed individually — no two cards are ever quite the same.
